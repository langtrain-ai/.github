# Code of conduct

This applies to every repository, issue, pull request and discussion in
[langtrain-ai](https://github.com/langtrain-ai).

We follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and keep
criticism about the work rather than the person. Harassment, personal attacks
and discriminatory language aren't tolerated.

## Reporting

Email **support@langtrain.xyz** (subject: "Code of conduct") with what happened, where, and any links.
Reports are read only by the maintainers and kept confidential. Maintainers may
remove content, or block anyone who breaks this code, from the organization.
